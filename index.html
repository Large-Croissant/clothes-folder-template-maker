Shirt Folder Template Maker

How it works
  1. Lay a shirt face-down on the center panel — it's the exact size of the folded result.
  2. Fold the left and right flaps in over the shirt.
  3. Fold the bottom flap up — and if you enabled bottom flap 2, fold that one over it for a double fold.
  4. Print the template, tape it to cardboard / foam board, cut it out.

Final folded size = center panel
